About STON.fi

STON.fi is a decentralized automated market maker (AMM) built on the TON blockchain. It provides near-zero trading fees, low slippage, an extremely simple interface, and direct integration with TON wallets.

Fully decentralized
STON.fi has no access to your coins/tokens and never asks for your personal information.

Near-zero trading fees
The TON blockchain offers much lower fees and higher transaction speed compared to Bitcoin or Ethereum.

Slippage close to zero
As an automated market maker system, STON.fi uses liquidity pools and simple but sophisticated algorithms to determine prices.
